# Successful Projects and Project Ideas
Back to [[PMIT1027]] · Prev: [[Team Dynamics]] · Next: [[The Project Pitch]]

## What makes projects succeed
| Factor | Example |
| --- | --- |
| **Clear scope, well-defined goals** | "Migrate **50 users** to Microsoft 365 by **Dec 15**" - not "we'll migrate our email" |
| **Stakeholder buy-in** | Sponsor attends weekly check-ins and clears roadblocks |
| **Realistic timeline and budget** | Walterdale Bridge - was the timeline realistic? |
| **Risk planning and adaptability** | A fallback "plan B" |
| **Communication and teamwork** | The Chicago Bulls - talent plus coordination |

## Why projects fail
* Vague or shifting goals (**scope creep**)
* Lack of communication
* Insufficient resources, planning or **testing**
* Poor risk management / no fallback
* Low engagement or **buy-in**

## Case studies
### ✅ Y2K
* Systems stored years as 2 digits (`99`) → risk of misreading `00` as 1900
* Huge scope (governments, businesses, utilities, hundreds of thousands of legacy systems), **non-negotiable deadline** (midnight Jan 1 2000)
* Success factors: **early inventory**, **prioritization by criticality**, **coordinated stakeholders**, **testing + fallback plans** (simulated rollovers)
* Outcome: few major outages - crisis averted

### ❌ Samsung Galaxy Note 7 (2016)
* Flagship phone; batteries **overheated and caught fire** - design/manufacturing flaws, no room for battery swelling, internal shorts
* Recall → **replacements also caught fire** → discontinued; **$5-6 billion** cost + brand damage
* Debate: poor design or poor project management? (Rushed timeline, insufficient testing and QC)

### ❓ Edmonton Walterdale Bridge
* Significantly **delayed** but stayed **on budget** (contractor paid penalties); now a civic landmark
* Is a late-but-on-budget, well-loved project a success? Success can be measured on several constraints

## Choosing a semester project
* **Research/planning based**, not a full build
* Feasible in a classroom (time, scope, resources)
* Clearly explainable and pitchable
* Relevant to IT/sysadmin is a plus
* Test: *"Could we create a solid plan for this, even if we don't carry it out?"*
* Activity: brainstorm 5 ideas → name 1-2 poor choices and why → narrow to 2 → message the instructor with team name, 2 ideas, and why each is viable
