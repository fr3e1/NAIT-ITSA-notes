# The Project Pitch
Back to [[PMIT1027]] · Prev: [[Successful Projects and Project Ideas]] · Next: [[Project Charters]]

## Why pitch?
* **Sell** the idea, gain **stakeholder buy-in**, build excitement
* Sets the foundation for the charter, scope, WBS...

## What a pitch is
* A **short persuasive story** about your idea
* Focused on the **why** and the big picture - not every technical detail
* Generates interest/support and is **memorable**

## Structure
1. **Hook** the audience
2. Define the **problem**
3. Present your **solution**
4. Highlight the **benefits**
5. **Call to action**

> Keep it short and focused. Storytelling beats a list of facts.

## Slide design
| ✅ Do | ❌ Avoid |
| --- | --- |
| High-quality images | Walls of text |
| Minimal text | Small fonts / clutter |
| Large clear fonts | Distracting animations |
| Visuals over words | Reading from slides |

## Delivery
* Eye contact, body language
* Natural, **not memorized**
* Engage the audience

### A weak pitch is
* Too vague · too technical · busy/confusing slides · no clear problem/solution

## Group coordination
* Assign speaking roles, **rehearse with a timer**, keep the flow consistent
* Use stories/examples, test slides, be smooth not robotic

## Audience check
* Do they understand the problem?
* Do they believe our solution?
* Will they remember us tomorrow?

## Practice
* Warm-up: 3 minutes to prepare a 1-minute pitch for a random object using Hook → Problem → Solution → Benefits → CTA
* Then a 1-minute mini pitch of your project idea
