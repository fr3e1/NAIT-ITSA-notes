# Team Norms Contract
Back to [[PMIT1027]]

> [!info] Purpose (5%)
> Speed up team development, increase **individual accountability**, and reduce conflict by agreeing on procedures, expectations and consequences **up front**. Complete, sign and submit as a team. Be as **specific** as possible: exact tasks, steps and people responsible.

## Sections
### 1. Team members
* Contact info, availability, skills each person brings

### 2. Team procedures
* Regular **meeting** day/time/place and expected attendance
* Preferred **communication** method and expected response time (e.g. Teams/Discord + shared docs)
* **Decision-making** policy (consensus, majority vote...)
* **Agendas** - who sets them, when, how people are reminded, who keeps the meeting on track
* **Record keeping** - who takes and shares minutes, where they're stored

### 3. Team expectations
* **Work quality** - realistic standards and target mark, effort each member can commit, strategies to meet them
* **Participation** - equal task distribution, preparation, updating drafts, including everyone's ideas (team maintenance), staying on task (task maintenance), tracking document changes, leadership style (informal/formal, individual/shared)
* **Personal accountability** - attendance/punctuality, responsibility for deadlines and quality, communication level, commitment to decisions

### 4. Consequences
* How the team handles infractions (poor quality, late work, missed meetings, not contributing)
* What happens if infractions continue (escalation path - e.g. documented warning → instructor)

> [!tip] When things go wrong
> Teams often hit **storming** after the contract is signed - that's normal ([[Team Dynamics#Tuckman's stages of team formation]]). Revisit the contract: is the team not following it, or does it need changing?
