# Project Pitch
Back to [[PMIT1027]]

> [!info] Overview (10%)
> A **10-minute** team pitch (PowerPoint/Prezi, live or recorded) uploaded to your private MS Teams channel; one member submits the link in a Word doc to Brightspace. Every member presents an **equal** portion.

## Expectations
* Minimal cue cards/script - **≥ 90% eye contact** with the camera
* Evidence of research and rehearsal; **sources cited on slides**
* **Professional look:** consistent theme, **≤ 5 elements per slide** (ideal = one full-screen image), text reduced to stats/facts/figures
* Dressy casual; no gum, hats or outerwear

## Structure
* **Introduction** - start strong, hook the audience, know your audience, find the **WIIFM** ("What's In It For Me")
* **Content**
	* Key points clear and supported; the project's **value** (sponsor/client perspective)
	* A **vision** that connects emotionally - a picture of success
	* Main **deliverables**
	* Rough **budget** and **timeline**, based on research/models/analysis/intuition
	* **KISS**, tell a story, no wimpy words ("I think...", "I believe...")
	* Answer **Why? Why now? Why me?**
* **Conclusion** - strong ending, reinforce the central idea and the advantage to the audience

## Rubric (/100)
| Criterion | Marks |
| --- | --- |
| PowerPoint design (quality imagery, no clutter) | 25 |
| Body language (90% eye contact) | 25 |
| Tone and inflection | 10 |
| No non-fluencies (ums/ahs) | 5 |
| Transitions between sections/presenters | 5 |
| Speed (~120 words/min, consistent) | 5 |
| Project value clear + connected to deliverables, urgency | 10 |
| Budget + timeline estimate supported by research | 10 |
| Follows the planned script | 5 |
*(approximate maxima from the rubric levels)*
