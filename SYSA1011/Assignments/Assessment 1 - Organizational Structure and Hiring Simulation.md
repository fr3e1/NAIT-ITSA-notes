# Assessment 1 - Organizational Structure and Hiring Simulation
Back to [[SYSA1011]]

> [!info] Overview (15%)
> Design a **fictional organization** and its **IT department**, evaluate three candidates for an open IT role and recommend one, then use **AI** to generate an org structure and critique it.

## Part 1 - Organizational structure design
* Pick an industry (healthcare, retail, education, manufacturing, logistics, gaming, MSP, finance, government, non-profit...)
* **Overview:** name, industry (+ province/country if regulations matter), approximate size, main operations (locations, products/services, key systems, planned growth, why invest in IT)
* **Role descriptions** for each IT role, most senior first: technical, operational/support, communication/collaboration, leadership/project responsibilities
* **Org chart** (Visio or similar) with **at least 5 IT roles**, showing:
	* Leadership and reporting relationships
	* Operational vs customer-facing roles
	* Specialized responsibilities
	* Cross-department communication
	* Realistic hierarchy
* Example roles: CIO, IT Manager, Systems Administrator, Helpdesk Technician, Network Administrator, Security Analyst, DBA, Cloud Administrator, Deployment Specialist

## Part 2 - Candidate evaluation and hiring recommendation (300-500 words)
* Choose one **non-management** position from your chart and describe:
	* Primary responsibilities
	* **Four** job-related selection criteria
	* At least **one technical qualification**
	* At least **one professional workplace expectation**
* Review the three provided resumes (**Alex Chen**, **Jordan Mitchell**, **Taylor Singh**) and recommend one:
	* Connect **resume evidence** to the requirements
	* **Compare** against the other two
	* Identify one **limitation / development need**
	* Explain why they're still the strongest choice
* **No predetermined correct candidate** - it's about the quality of the justification and fit with *your* organization

| Candidate | Profile (parallels class practice) |
| --- | --- |
| Jordan Mitchell | Helpdesk/customer service experience, user-facing (≈ Morgan Lee) |
| Alex Chen | Homelab, virtualization, Windows Server/Linux, cloud cert, less workplace experience (≈ Priya Patel) |
| Taylor Singh | Student volunteer/mentor, teamwork and adaptability, lighter technical depth (≈ Diego Ramirez) |

## Part 3 - AI-assisted organizational review
* Use any AI (ChatGPT, Copilot, Gemini, Claude...) to generate an org chart/structure for your company
* Deliver: **tool used**, **1-3 best prompts**, **screenshot/export** of the output
* Evaluate:
	* What was useful/effective?
	* What inaccuracies, limitations or weaknesses?
	* What did you change manually?
	* **Which version would you present to senior leadership, and why?**

## Submission
* One professional package: overview, org chart, role descriptions, hiring recommendation, AI review (the class Word template has placeholders for each)

## Tips
* Justify with **evidence and reasoning, not preference** ([[Hiring Decisions and Candidate Evaluation]])
* Match the structure to the organization's size and needs ([[Organizational Structure and IT Roles]])
* A healthcare org needs privacy/compliance roles; an MSP needs customer-facing service roles; growth plans justify roles you add later
