## The decision framework (used all course)
1. **Identify** the issue
2. **Consider the impact** - who is affected?
3. **Determine** an appropriate action
4. **Consider the impact of that action** - if it isn't appropriate, go back to 3
5. **Implement, communicate and/or escalate**
