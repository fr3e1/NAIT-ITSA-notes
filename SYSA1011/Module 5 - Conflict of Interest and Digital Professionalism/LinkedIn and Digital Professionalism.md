# LinkedIn and Digital Professionalism
Back to [[SYSA1011]] · Prev: [[Conflict of Interest and Disclosure]] · Next: [[Organizational IT Resources and Acceptable Use]]

## The hiring landscape
* **Resume review → LinkedIn review → Online presence review → Interview screening → References**
* Many employers review you **before** deciding to interview
* What comes up when someone searches your name? LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, Reddit... or nothing?

## Digital professionalism
How you present yourself online in professional environments:
1. **LinkedIn** - primary professional profile
2. **Professional communication** - email, messaging, online interactions
3. **Public social media** - visible to employers/colleagues
4. **Portfolio websites**
5. **Professional communities** - forums, groups, industry platforms

## LinkedIn is not social media
* It's **professional networking**, a **recruiting platform**, a **professional portfolio**, a **career development tool** - not your personal life
* Employers use it to: verify experience, search for candidates, review interests/engagement, evaluate communication, build talent pipelines

## A strong profile
| Component | Tips |
| --- | --- |
| **Photo** | Clear, current, face visible, appropriate attire, clean background (no photographer needed) |
| **Banner** | Professional/tech themed |
| **Headline** | Weak: "Student" → Strong: *"IT Student \| Systems Administration \| Windows & Linux \| Seeking IT Support Opportunities"* - often the first thing recruiters see |
| **About** | Who you are, what you're studying, what interests you, what you're seeking - **future direction, not life story** |
| **Experience** | Include projects, labs, volunteer work, home labs, customer service, troubleshooting - you have more than you think |
| **Skills** | Match postings (see [[Resume Workshop and Job Posting Analysis]]) |
| **Education** | Program, school, dates |
| **Custom URL** | `linkedin.com/in/firstname-lastname` instead of the default |

* Beyond LinkedIn: **GitHub**, portfolio site, Indeed, industry associations, tech communities

## Professional or personal?
| Content | Where |
| --- | --- |
| Frustrations about your employer | ❌ Personal (or nowhere) |
| Technical certifications | ✅ LinkedIn |
| Political opinions | ❌ Personal |
| Project demonstrations | ✅ LinkedIn |
| Career achievements | ✅ LinkedIn |

## Scenario
* A recruiter finds an **incomplete LinkedIn**, a **professional GitHub**, and **public offensive posts** → a digital footprint **can disqualify an otherwise strong candidate**; a good GitHub doesn't necessarily offset it

## Managing your online presence
1. **Identify** what's publicly visible
2. **Consider impact** - how would employers/colleagues see it?
3. **Determine action** - update, remove, improve
4. **Communicate** - how do you want to present yourself?

## Key takeaways
* Employers evaluate you **before** interviews
* LinkedIn is **professional networking**, not social media
* Digital professionalism directly affects opportunities
