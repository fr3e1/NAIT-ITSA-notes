## Factors in hiring
* **Technical qualifications** - skills, certifications, experience
* **Professional qualities** - communication, teamwork, reliability, professionalism
* Hiring = **balancing factors**, not finding a perfect candidate. Technically excellent + poor communicator = friction; personable + underprepared = technical risk

## Organizational fit
* A good hire depends on **goals**, **team requirements**, **technical responsibilities**, **culture**

| Startup may prioritize | Large government org may prioritize |
| --- | --- |
| Adaptability | Process |
| Willingness to learn | Documentation |
| | Consistency |

## Reading a job requirement
* Sysadmin responsibilities: manage servers, support users, maintain backups, monitor health, **document changes**
* Reverse-engineer: which **3 technical** and **3 professional** strengths would someone need?

## Resume red flags
* Spelling errors · missing info · vague descriptions · unprofessional formatting · lack of relevant experience
* Some are **dealbreakers**, others are **coachable** - context matters (a typo means more for a technical writer)

## What resumes show (and don't)
* Show: technical skills, certifications, experience, communication style, attention to detail, presentation
* A resume shows what a candidate **claims**, not how they'll **perform** - hiring needs interpretation and judgment

## Evaluation framework
1. What **stands out**?
2. What **strengths** are demonstrated?
3. What **concerns** do you notice?
4. What **questions** would you ask?

* Strongest technically? Most workplace-ready? Most growth potential? **Would the answer change by organization?**

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **There is no perfect candidate**
> Organizations may prioritize technical depth, communication, customer service, leadership or growth potential. **Strong recommendations are supported with evidence and reasoning, not personal preference** - point to specific resume content.

## Reflection
* For a team you've been on: which qualities matter most, and **which is hardest to teach after hiring?** (Usually attitude/communication - technical skills are the most teachable)

## Key takeaways
* Hiring involves more than technical skills
* Organizational needs shape selection
* Professional qualities are workplace assets
* Recommendations need evidence and justification
