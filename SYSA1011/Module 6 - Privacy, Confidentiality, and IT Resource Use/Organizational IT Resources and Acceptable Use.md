# Organizational IT Resources and Acceptable Use
Back to [[SYSA1011]] · Prev: [[Privacy, Confidentiality and Workplace Trust]] · Next: [[Communication and Active Listening]]

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Organizational resources are **entrusted** to you, not **given** to you.

## Stewardship
* A **steward** is entrusted with resources that belong to someone else - you may **use**, **manage** and **control** them, but **you don't own them**
* Day one you get a laptop, phone, M365 account, VPN, licences, badge - the laptop *feels* personal but is organizational property
* **Misuse doesn't require theft** - personal use of company time, unauthorized software, shared credentials

### The stewardship test (highest risk?)
* Physical assets feel obvious, but **company time** and **company data** are the most misused and hardest to detect

## Resources are more than hardware
| Physical | Digital | Organizational |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Laptops | Accounts | Employee **time** |
| Phones | Software licences | Budgets |
| Servers | Cloud services | **Reputation** |
| Network equipment | **Data** | Intellectual property |

## Acceptable Use Policies (AUPs)
* Define expectations for **technology use**, **software installation**, **security responsibilities**, **internet usage**, **data handling**, **monitoring**
* Protect **both** the employee and the organization
* Assume your activity on company resources is **visible/monitored**

## Framework (same as ethics, conflict of interest, privacy)
**Identify the issue → Consider the impact → Determine an appropriate action → Communicate and escalate**

## Scenarios
### Personal software (Steam, Discord, games after hours) on a company laptop - policy prohibits it
* Security vulnerabilities · IT can't support unapproved software · policy violation · erodes trust · licensing concerns
* > Professional decisions are judged by **policy and risk**, not intention

### One licence shared by five employees ("saves money")
* Licence agreement violations have **legal and financial** consequences; audits → penalties
* Who's accountable? **Saving money doesn't override obligations** - request proper licences instead

### Confidential project info pasted into a public AI chatbot
* Excellent report, no reported breach - but data **left the organization's control** (may be retained/used for training)
* Approved/enterprise AI tool ≠ public tool
* > A tool being useful doesn't make its use appropriate. **No policy ≠ permission** - err on the side of caution

### Side business printing on the company printer
* Gradual after-hours printing of invoices/labels/promo → **~$20,000** in paper, toner, maintenance, wear. Nobody noticed
* Still misuse - most resource misuse happens **gradually**, not in one dramatic event

### Workshop - former employee, left 6 months ago, still has active VPN, M365 and building access
* **Identify:** orphaned accounts = serious security risk
* **Impact:** unauthorized access to data/network/building; compliance failure
* **Action:** disable all access now (per procedure), check logs for use since departure
* **Communicate/escalate:** report to supervisor/security; fix the **offboarding process** gap (HR ↔ IT)

## Reflection
* What do organizations trust IT professionals with most? **Data, privileged access, organizational trust, time**
