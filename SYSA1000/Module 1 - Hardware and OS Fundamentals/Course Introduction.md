# Course Introduction
Back to [[SYSA1000]] · Next: [[Hardware Identification and Handling]]

* **SYSA1000 - Computer Fundamentals** - "where the hardware you can touch meets the systems you will build"
* The only ITSA course that deals with **physical hardware** - you hold a CPU before you configure a vCPU

## How the course runs
* Two classes a week: one **virtual**, one in the **computer lab**
* **Bring your laptop to every class** - lab machines are the hardware being worked on, your laptop is where you record findings
* Everything graded lives in **Brightspace** (content, assignments, rubrics, grades) - check before every class

## ITSA SharePoint
* https://naitca.sharepoint.com/sites/ITSAProgram
* **General Documents** - program policies and procedures, including the **APA template** for reports
* **Course Materials** - files too big for Brightspace (VMs, ISOs)

## Getting help
* Ask during class - questions are expected
* Every assignment document says exactly what it wants
