Usually I think of cookies as sending your psersonal information to the people who owns the site, but this is not the case. It's more that in the session section of the OSI Model / process, whenever you send information to the website owner, they send you back a cookie tracker that confirms that those information belongs to this device, which allows them to monitor or supply you with logged in sessions. 

But still... don't accept no cookies... those information are yours and yours only.
