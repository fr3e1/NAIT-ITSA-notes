TCP is secure and direct but takes a longer time, but in return you have less packet loss. UDP is the oposite where you're sending out packets in a form of beacon where anyone and everyone can technically receive this data and there's no garrantee of the packet reaching it's destination.

So instead of imagining it as a line of code, I got a better way to think of this. Imagine you're a bank and you're trying ot wire transfer money. You can either wire transfer it with encryption and over a secure portal, which is slow but ensures that the money gets to the person (TCP) or, you coulf hand it over to a mail man and hope for the best (UDP).

These two are basically your method of transportation.
